These are the page-table entries printed out by pgtbltest.
First, the virual address (va) is printed on the left end.
Then, the page table entry (pte) associated with that va is printed.
Following that are the physical address (pa) and permissions (perm),
which are extracted from the pte. The pa is the physical address
of the beginning of the page that the pte points to.

The first pte has permissions of 0x5B, or 0101 1011 in binary.  R-XU
This page contains the program code.

The second pte has permissions of 0x1B, or 0001 1011 in binary. R-XU
This page contains program data.

The third pte has permissions of 0x17, or 0001 0111 in binary.  RW-U
This is the Heap.

The fourth pte has permissions of 0x7, or 0000 0111 in binary.  RW--
This page cannot be used by the user. If the stack grows larger than a
page, then it will overflow into this page and the program will crash.

The fifth pte has permissions of 0xD7, or 1101 0111 in binary.  RW-U
This is the Stack.

All of the ptes in the middle have 0x0 permissions.
These pages are not valid, they cannot be used by anyone for
anything, and attempting to access them will cause a page fault.

The second to last pte has permissions 0xC7, or 1100 0111 in binary. RW--
This is the trapframe page.

The last pte has has permissions 0x4B, or 0100 1011 in binary.  R-X-
This is the trampoline page.
